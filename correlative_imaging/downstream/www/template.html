Thick outline = the refined patched cell (grown from its seed, neighbours competing for pixels), coloured by its calibrated call (white = uncertain / dim). In the hole: solid = stringent (whole-image threshold), dashed = lenient (hole threshold); thick = candidate, thin dotted = dropped (weak / noise / "bt" bleed-through). Number = P(real cell, not noise). Grey fill + grey dashed outline = the whole neighbour cell behind a rim piece. Thin outlines = whole extent of hole particles; away from the hole = field particles.
